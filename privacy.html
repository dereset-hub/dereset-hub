Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you visit dereset.com or purchase a DeReset digital guide. DeReset publishes short, practical digital guides (PDF) and companion tools, sold through Gumroad.

1. Information from browsing dereset.com

The DeReset website (dereset.com and its product pages) is a static site. It does not use accounts, does not set cookies of its own, and does not collect information through forms, because it has none.

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall visit traffic. Cloudflare Web Analytics is a cookie-free, privacy-focused analytics service that does not track individual visitors across sites or build advertising profiles. Cloudflare, Inc. acts as a data processor for this limited analytics function. You can read Cloudflare's own privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

The site does not use third-party advertising trackers or pixels.

2. Information from a purchase

All DeReset products are sold and delivered through Gumroad, a separate platform, at our storefront (dereset.gumroad.com). When you buy a DeReset guide, your purchase — including your email address and payment details — is handled entirely by Gumroad, off the dereset.com domain. Gumroad acts as the merchant of record for these sales and as an independent data processor for your payment and order information.

We do not receive or store your payment card details. Gumroad may contact you directly regarding your order, receipt, and product delivery. Please review Gumroad's own privacy policy for details on how it handles your information: gumroad.com/privacy.

3. What we do with your information

If you email us at support@dereset.com — for a question, a refund request, or anything else — we use that email only to respond to you and to process your request. We do not sell your information to third parties.

4. Children's privacy

DeReset's products and website are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age required by applicable law to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our products, tools, or hosting arrangements change. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of dereset.com or purchase of a DeReset product after an update means you accept the revised policy.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to support@dereset.com. See also our Contact page.
